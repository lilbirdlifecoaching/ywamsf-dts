About YWAM San Francisco

We believe God loves San Francisco.

We believe God is pursuing real, transformational relationship with each and every person in our city, and we want to be a part of what God is already doing.

Our mission

Our mission is to engage San Francisco with a loving God.

We have a vision to birth unique, focused ministry throughout San Francisco. In our city, mission is at our doorstep.

How we engage the city

Seven ways we show up.

  1. Having influence in significant places
  2. Creating opportunities for community involvement in urban missions
  3. Collaborating with local churches and agencies
  4. Supporting complete individual development
  5. Advocating for vulnerable populations
  6. Using creative means to share faith
  7. Reaching unreached populations with citywide impact
  8. In our city, mission is at our doorstep.
357 Ellis Street

Mission is at our doorstep.

Our home is a 100-year-old ministry center in the heart of the Tenderloin. Every week its doors open for the Ellis Room, the food pantry, showers and haircuts, Bible study and Pop-Up Church on the sidewalk.

It is also where we train. Students in our Discipleship Training School and the outreach teams who join us live, eat and worship here, a few steps from the neighbors we serve.
