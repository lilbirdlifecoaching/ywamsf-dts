Restoration initiatives · tap a card

Restore. Empower. Serve.

i.

Restore

Our heart is for people to be restored to God, their families and others: restored dignity and restored identity.

ii.

Empower

We equip people with tools for a stable life, to pursue work, employment and housing, and to be their true selves.

iii.

Serve

First by being good listeners. Free haircuts, showers and prayer. We go with people to the hospital, court and rehab.

Volunteer with us
Need food?

The food pantry, every Thursday.

We partner with the SF-Marin Food Bank. Sign-ups happen on site at 357 Ellis Street, Thursdays at 3:30 pm. We serve residents of the 94102 zip code: bring an ID with your current address, or your ID and a recent piece of mail.

Need food right away? Visit the SF-Marin Food Bank or call 211.
