Church on the Street

May 4, 2026 · YWAM SF Communications

Every Sunday morning, right on the street, people gather together to praise and worship God. Half of the street is closed, and chairs line there, conversations begin on the sidewalk, a table with cookies and hot chocolate is ready to serve those who walk by, and songs of hope fill the air. Then the Pop-Up Church starts.

In the middle of the city, surrounded by everyday life, we see lives being touched, stories being shared, and hearts being restored. There are no walls, no barriers, just people coming together to encounter God right where they are. Some come searching, some come curious, and others come carrying heavy burdens. But every week, something changes. There is joy. There is peace. There is purpose.

This is more than a church service; it’s a mission field. It’s a reminder that the Gospel is meant to be lived out in the open, among people, right in the heart of the community. And the incredible part? You can be part of it.

If you’ve ever felt the desire for something more purpose, more impact, more adventure, this is your invitation. Our Mission Adventure Program is designed for those who want to step out of the ordinary and into a life that truly makes a difference. Imagine being equipped, trained, and sent out to bring hope not just here, but wherever God leads you.

Come experience it for yourself every Sunday morning. Sit with us, listen, connect, and see what God is doing. And if your heart is stirred, take the next step into the Missions Adventure.

Your journey could start right here… on the street.
