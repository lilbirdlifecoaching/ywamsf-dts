Great Commission

Jul 9, 2025 · YWAM SF Communications

Great Commission is to go and make disciples of all nations. School of Transformational Business is an intensive six-week course. Where you will learn, how to start and manage a godly, profitable business to extend the Kingdom of God in society and to the unreached nations. SOTB is designed for those passionate about making God known by doing business to fulfill the Great Commission.

We have experienced entrepreneurs who have participated in Business as Mission and professionals with business management experience. You will learn how to use business to reach people in different cultures.

Great Commission is a call of every believer. We are part of BWAM of Youth with a Mission that organizes, trains and establishes networks to take the Gospel to the less reached places through business. This school is registered at the University of the Nations as a Seminar for six weeks and those who have done a DTS will get UofN credit.

SOTB is also designed for entrepreneurs who need to apply biblical principles to their businesses to accomplish the Great Commission. It also encourages Christian businessmen to become more actively involved in the Great Commission.

Apply today for our upcoming SOTB at YWAM San Francisco. Please contact us if you have any questions.
