The Great Commission

Oct 4, 2025 · YWAM SF Communications

Do you believe business can be a tool for building the Kingdom of God? Business can be more than profit. It can bring hope, justice, and lasting transformation.

School of Transformational Business (SOTB) will help you learn how to integrate faith, leadership, and entrepreneurship. We want to bring real transformation in the marketplace and beyond. Whether you’re an entrepreneur, student, or professional, this school will help you align your business calling with God’s mission.

You will discover how to use your entrepreneurial gifts to advance the Kingdom of God in every sphere of society. For six weeks, students from different nations and professional backgrounds will gather together in the heart of San Francisco. We will dive deep into what it means to do business God’s way.

There will be interactive lectures, hands-on projects, and mentorship from experienced entrepreneurs and mission leaders. We teach you how business can be a powerful tool for transformation, both economically and socially, as well as spiritually. Students will also have opportunities to engage directly with the community in Tenderloin. Business principles can bring hope, dignity, and long-term solutions to issues such as poverty and unemployment.

Join us at YWAM San Francisco for this great opportunity. Starting from July 13 for 6 weeks. And explore how faith and business can come together to impact the world for the Kingdom of God.

💡 What You’ll Discover:
✨ Foundations of BWAM — Business with a Mission
✨ Integrating Faith & Work — Breaking the sacred vs. secular divide
✨ Character & Leadership — Lead with integrity, not greed
✨ Marketing & Finance — Kingdom principles with practical tools
✨ Business Plan Presentation — Develop and pitch your God-inspired idea

This 6-week course will help you build a business that serves people, honors God, and changes the world.

Please contact us if you would like to learn more about this school.

Apply today at ywamsanfrancisco.org/sotb
