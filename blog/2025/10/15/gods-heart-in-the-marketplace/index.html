God's heart in the Marketplace

Oct 15, 2025 · YWAM SF Communications

School Of Transformational Business (SOTB)

The School of Transformational Business (SOTB) teaches people to start and run a godly, profitable business. We aim to bring God’s kingdom to earth through every business. It is designed for those who have a passion for knowing God and making him known through business. It is for those who have an interest in fulfilling the Great Commission to the nations.

SOTB will offer teaching from experienced business owners from different parts of the world. Those who have been involved in Business with A Mission and have extensive experience in business. We will also teach how to use business to reach people of different cultures.

Some topics will cover:

During the lecture, you will have the opportunity to develop a step-by-step business plan for your business. We also teach you how to make a Kingdom Plan. We walk with you in every process and help you to start up a business and continue to build His kingdom.

  • BWAM Vision and Values,

  • Biblical Foundation,

  • History, Introduction to Business, and Mission Plan.

  • Traditional Mindsets & Sacred vs. Secular.

  • Integration and Workplace Discipleship Skills.

  • Roots and Heart of Biblical Marketing.

  • Value People and Relationships.

  • Essentials of Basic Marketing.

  • Budget making, Break-even Point,

  • Basic Accounting and Financial Forecasting

  • The spiritual power of money, etc.
