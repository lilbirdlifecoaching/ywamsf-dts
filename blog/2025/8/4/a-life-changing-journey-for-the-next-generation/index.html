A Life-Changing Journey for the Next Generation

What if one week could change your life forever?
That’s the heartbeat of Mission Adventures, a high-impact, short-term mission trip designed specifically for youth groups, students, and young adults who want to step out of their comfort zone and into God’s heart for the world.

At YWAM San Francisco, Mission Adventures is more than just a service trip. It’s a fully guided, immersive discipleship and outreach experience. Our team handles the logistics, allowing youth leaders to focus on their students and students to focus on hearing God’s voice, serving the city, and growing deeper in their faith.

💥 What Happens During Mission Adventures?

  • Team training: Before you hit the streets, we equip you with powerful teaching, worship, prayer, and team-building.

  • Local outreach: From serving the unhoused in the Tenderloin to street evangelism, food distribution, and creative ministry, students encounter real people, real stories, and the real presence of God.

  • Urban immersion: Learn about justice, poverty, missions, and how to live boldly for Jesus in today’s culture.

  • Community living: Experience a taste of missionary life right here in San Francisco.

🌍 Why San Francisco?

San Francisco is one of the most diverse, challenging, and spiritually hungry cities in the world. It's a place where faith meets reality and young people discover that mission isn’t just overseas, it’s right next door.

✨ What Students Walk Away With:

  • Confidence in sharing their faith

  • A deeper understanding of God's heart for people

  • Lasting friendships

  • A call to live missionally wherever they go next

🙌 Ready to Join?

Whether you're a youth pastor looking for a turnkey mission experience or a student hungry to grow, Mission Adventures is a game-changer. Let this be the trip that sparks a lifetime of mission.

👉 Learn more and sign up today: https://www.ywamsanfrancisco.org/outreach
