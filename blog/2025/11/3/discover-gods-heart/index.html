Discover God’s Heart

Nov 10, 2025 · YWAM SF Communications

Have you ever longed to know God more deeply and to share His love with a world in need? That’s what the Discipleship Training School (DTS) is all about. Discover God’s heart in the City. The journey of a DTS in San Francisco.

At YWAM San Francisco, right in the heart of the Tenderloin, DTS is a five-month discipleship journey that challenges, equips, and sends you to the nations with the Gospel. It’s a life-changing season of encountering God, growing in community, and learning to see the world through His eyes.

💡 Lecture Phase - know God

For the first three months, you’ll dive into powerful teachings that build your faith and shape your character. You’ll live and learn in the middle of one of the most diverse and challenging neighborhoods in California, a place where God’s love meets real human need every day.

Weekly topics include:

  • Hearing God’s Voice

  • The Father Heart of God

  • The Nature and Character of God

  • Destiny by Design

  • Evangelism and Missions

Each day combines worship, teaching, small groups, and hands-on ministry right here in the Tenderloin community. It’s not just about knowing more, it’s about being transformed.

🌍 Outreach Phase - make God Known

After three months of training, you’ll step out for two months of outreach locally and internationally. You’ll take what you’ve learned and share it with others through evangelism, mercy ministry, prayer, and service. Whether serving on the streets of San Francisco or in a village overseas, you’ll see the Gospel come alive through your own hands and heart.

❤️ Why DTS in San Francisco?

San Francisco is a city of contrasts, beauty and brokenness, wealth and need, creativity and searching. Doing DTS here means following Jesus in a place that challenges your comfort zone and expands your compassion.

You’ll:

  • Deepen your relationship with God.

  • Learn to love your neighbor in real, tangible ways.

  • Discover your calling in God’s global mission.

This is where discipleship meets the streets, where faith becomes action. And where God transforms hearts, including yours.

📅 Join the Next DTS

If you’re ready to know God and make Him known, come join us. Experience five months that could change the rest of your life. The DTS starts on January 26, 2026.
