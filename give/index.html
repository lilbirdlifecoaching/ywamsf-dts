You make this possible.

Your gift does so many things, from keeping the lights on to providing necessary resources and community to neighbors.

Give online

Give once or monthly.

Give by credit card through Click & Pledge. Monthly gifts help us plan ahead for the pantry, showers and outreach.

  • Click & Pledge charges a small fee per transaction.
  • Your receipt comes by email. Tax receipts are issued to U.S. residents only.
  • YWAM San Francisco is a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Tax ID #94-3218675.
  • Giving to a staff member’s ministry? You can give here too. Gifts for students can’t be sent to YWAM SF directly.

We’re financially accredited by national accountability organizations: the Evangelical Council for Financial Accountability (ECFA) and GuideStar.

Questions? Email info@ywamsanfrancisco.org or call 415-885-6543.

In-kind gifts

What we need right now.

To give any of these, call 415-885-6543 or email office@ywamsanfrancisco.org first, so we can plan where it goes.

For the Ellis Room

  • Disposable razors and shaving cream
  • Travel-sized toiletries (toothbrushes, toothpaste, floss, shampoo, conditioner, lotion, sunscreen, deodorant, moisturizer)
  • Combs and brushes
  • Casual shoes (sneakers, boots)
  • New or like-new clothing (please, no used socks or undergarments)
  • Accessories (hats, beanies, belts, backpacks, tote bags)
  • Blankets
  • Billiard equipment (cues, green chalk)
  • Board games, chess or checker sets
  • Coffee percolators (100-cup capacity)

Practical needs

  • An industrial set of knives
  • Office chairs (sets of 3 or more)
  • Laptop computers
  • Piano tuning

Housing needs

  • Silverware and cooking utensils
  • Dishware (mugs, glasses, plates, bowls)
  • Cookware (pots, pans)
  • New bedding sets (twin or full)
  • New pillows
  • Cutting boards
  • Microwaves
  • Toasters

Give your time, too.
