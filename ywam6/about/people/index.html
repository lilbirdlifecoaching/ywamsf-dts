Board of directors

Guiding and stewarding the ministry.

Our board brings decades of experience in missions, leadership and finance.

Tim Svoboda

President · YWAM San Francisco Bay Area Coordinator

An International Cities Coordinator for YWAM, Tim served in India from 1983 to 2007 as National Director of YWAM India and Chennai City Coordinator, and founded the Chennai Transformation Network. He holds a Doctor of Ministry from Bakke Graduate University, where he also teaches.

John Briggs

Chair of the Board · COO, Mission Builders International

John joined YWAM Montana with his wife Donna in 1984 and served there for 15 years. In 2000 he founded Mission Builders International, which places short-term volunteers in more than 1,200 locations across 186 nations.

Jason Martinez

Board Secretary · Finance Director

Jason completed his DTS in San Francisco in 2005 and has served across the base ever since, overseeing the office, communications, kitchen, maintenance, hospitality and finances. He and Justine have three children.

Shani Mahany

Chair of the Audit Committee

Director of Finance at Sacred Heart Cathedral Preparatory, Shani is a CPA, Certified Fraud Examiner and Certified in Financial Forensics. She is married with three children.

Gabrielle Alzate

Executive Director, Because Justice Matters

Gabrielle joined YWAM in 2010, launched a youth ministry in the Tenderloin and now leads Because Justice Matters, advocating for women and girls in San Francisco. She holds a master’s in Christian Community Development.

Gordy McDonald

YWAM Montana Leadership Development

A youth pastor from 1973, Gordy joined YWAM in Kona in 1979, led YWAM Montana until 2002 and served as Northwest Director over Idaho, Washington, Oregon, Alaska and Wyoming.

Kirsten Markley

Board Member

A senior leader and consultant who builds strategy and cross-functional teams, Kirsten is passionate about innovation, creative environments and serving her community.
