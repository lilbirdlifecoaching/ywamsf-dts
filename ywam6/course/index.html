DTS · The 5 Month Course · Jan 18 – Jun 4, 2027

Do you find yourself longing for more?

More from life. More of Jesus. More of the world beyond your own.

A Discipleship Training School (DTS) is five months set apart to know God and to make Him known. Three months of training here in the Tenderloin, two months of outreach in the cities of the world, then a debrief to send you into whatever comes next.

We go to the edges, to the people most of the world walks past, not as heroes or fixers but as learners and neighbors. We listen first. We serve alongside local churches who were there long before us and will be there long after. And again and again we find that Jesus was already there too.

A monastery without walls

Live amid the chaos. Stay grounded in His presence.

Monks used to build their monasteries far from the noise. We built ours in the middle of it. Sirens, shouting on the corner, a neighbor who needs to talk: the city doesn’t go quiet for us.

So we keep a rhythm that holds. Morning prayer before the street wakes. One shared table. Silence, Scripture and work done with our hands. Over five months you learn what the old monks knew: stillness isn’t somewhere you go. It’s Someone you stay close to, wherever you are.

From the neighborhood to the nations.

i.

Training

ii.

Outreach

About 2 months · the cities of the world

FAQ
Do I need to have my faith figured out?

No. Honest questions are welcome. We’d love to hear your story and help you discern your next step.

Can I work or go to school during DTS?

We don’t recommend it. DTS is time set aside to deepen your relationship with God, and your capacity will already be stretched.

Is the Tenderloin safe?

It’s a real neighborhood with real struggles. You’ll never serve alone, and you’ll learn from staff who have lived and served here for years.

What happens after DTS?

The debrief sets you up for your next season: a job, college, joining YWAM, or wherever God is leading.

The door is open

How do I know I’m ready for a DTS?

You don’t have to figure it out alone. Start wherever feels right.
