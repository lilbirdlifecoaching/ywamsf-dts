DTS · Application · Jan 18 – Jun 4, 2027

Apply for the DTS

Applications are open for the January 2027 school, with 15 spots.

Before you start
  • Set aside about 30–40 minutes. There are 7 short sections.
  • Have ready: your passport details, your pastor’s or church leader’s contact details, an emergency contact and your health insurance information.
  • The last section asks how you plan to cover lecture and outreach fees. No payment is taken here.

Questions? Email dts@ywamsanfrancisco.org or call 415-885-6543.

Not ready to apply yet? Send us an inquiry and a DTS staff member will be in touch.
