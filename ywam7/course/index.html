A Discipleship Training School (DTS) is five months to grow in your relationship with God by putting your faith into practice: three months of training here in the Tenderloin, two months of outreach in the cities of the world, then a debrief to send you into whatever comes next.

This is discipleship with your feet on the ground. You’ll learn alongside people who have spent years serving this neighborhood, and discover what it means to live the way of Jesus in the everyday moments of life.

We’re becoming a community shaped by the way of Jesus, whose presence brings peace to the people and the place around it. Over five months you learn that stillness isn’t somewhere you go. It’s Someone you stay close to, and carry with you wherever you are.

Five months is a long time to be somewhere new. Here’s the place you’ll call home.

The city

San Francisco packs fog, hills, ocean and dozens of neighborhoods into 47 square miles. On days off, students head for the Golden Gate, Ocean Beach, Chinatown and the trails of the Presidio.

The building

357 Ellis Street has been our home since 1995. The Ellis Room fills the ground floor: coffee, pool, conversation and a welcome for anyone who walks in.

Where you’ll live

You’ll live at 357 Ellis with the rest of your school, sharing rooms, chores and the everyday stuff of community. Staff are on site, so help is never far away.

Where you’ll eat

Meals are planned and shared at the base, around one table with YWAM staff. Everyone takes a turn in the kitchen and at the sink.

Where you’ll learn

Teaching happens in our classroom on site, with a different speaker each week. Then you work it out on the street, in small groups and over coffee with your mentor.

San Francisco is a city of breathtaking beauty, rich culture and striking contrasts. In the heart of it lies the Tenderloin: often overlooked, and full of people, stories and opportunities for connection. We’re honest about the hard parts.

The neighborhood

The Tenderloin sits in the middle of downtown, a few blocks from Union Square. It’s home to families, immigrants, seniors and neighbors walking through hard seasons. It’s loud and it’s real, and it’s where you’ll learn your neighbors by name.

What you’ll see

  • Neighbors living on the street, in single-room hotels and in crisis.
  • Addiction, mental illness and poverty, up close and by name.
  • Noise, mess and a city that doesn’t slow down.
  • Grief and hard stories, alongside a lot of joy, humor and resilience.

What holds you

  • You never serve alone: always in teams, alongside staff who know these blocks.
  • A weekly mentor, a small group and a community that prays together.
  • Rhythms of rest: Sabbath, free time and days off to explore the city.
  • A purpose: becoming a steady, caring presence in the lives of people others pass by.
Do I need to have my faith figured out?

No. Honest questions are welcome. We’d love to hear your story and help you discern your next step.

Can I have my own room?

No. Community living is part of the package. You’ll share a room with a few other students in an apartment on site.

Does the $8,000 cover my travel to San Francisco?

No. You’re responsible for organizing your own travel to San Francisco, whether you’re coming from across the country or from overseas.

How long is the DTS?

About five months: around three months of training here in San Francisco, about two months of outreach in cities around the world, then a few days of debrief.

What does it cost, and do I have to pay it all myself?

The school is $8,000 USD. We believe finances shouldn’t stop someone from saying yes: most students raise some or all of it through friends, family and churches, and our team will help you explore fundraising and support options. Cost may vary for international applicants; please ask about alternative pricing.

Do I need ministry experience?

No. You don’t need years of ministry experience. You do need a heart that’s ready to learn, a faith you’re willing to put into practice and a desire to love people well.

Can I keep working or studying during DTS?

No. DTS is a full-time commitment for the whole school, so there isn’t room for a job or classes alongside it. That’s true for people who already live in San Francisco too.

Is the Tenderloin safe?

It’s a real neighborhood with real struggles. You’ll never serve alone, and you’ll learn from staff who have lived and served here for years.

What happens after DTS?

The debrief sets you up for your next season: a job, college, joining YWAM, or wherever God is leading.

What happens on outreach?

You travel as a team for about two months to put what you’ve learned into practice, serving alongside local churches and ministries in cities around the world.

How do I apply?

Apply online. Set aside about 30–40 minutes: there are 7 short sections, and it helps to have your passport details, a pastor’s or church leader’s contact details, an emergency contact and your health insurance information ready. Not ready yet? Send an inquiry first and a DTS staff member will be in touch.

Still wondering about something? Ask in the chat, or email dts@ywamsanfrancisco.org.

Who will be looking out for my son or daughter?

Every student is part of a small group led by our DTS staff and meets one-on-one with a staff mentor each week. Our staff teach, eat and serve alongside students every day, and several have lived and served on Ellis Street for years.

Is the Tenderloin safe?

It’s a real neighborhood with real struggles, and we don’t pretend otherwise. Students never do street ministry alone, always go out in teams with staff, and learn from people who know these blocks well. Most of all, we teach them to see neighbors, not danger.

Where will they live and eat?

Students live together at our base at 357 Ellis Street and share meals there with staff. See the “Your home” tab above for a look at the building, the kitchen and the neighborhood.

How will we stay in touch?

Students can keep in touch with home throughout the school. Before outreach begins, we let families know where teams are going and how to reach them while they’re away.

Who is YWAM San Francisco?

We’ve run Discipleship Training Schools in San Francisco since 1991, and at 357 Ellis Street since 1996. We’re part of Youth With A Mission, a global, interdenominational Christian movement with locations in more than 180 nations.

How do we help with the cost?

Most students raise some or all of their fees through friends, family and churches. Our team will walk your student through fundraising, and you’re welcome to ask us about payment options too.

Is this for you?

You don’t need years of ministry experience.

You do need a heart that’s ready to learn, a faith you’re willing to put into practice, and a desire to love people well.

If you’re serious about following Jesus, exploring a call to ministry, or wanting to understand what it means to love your neighbor in practical, everyday ways, we’d love to hear from you.
