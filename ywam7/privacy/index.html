Privacy policy

Your privacy

Last updated November 2026

This policy explains how YWAM San Francisco collects, uses, keeps and shares information from people who use ywamsanfrancisco.org and the programs and services offered on it.

The short version

  • We collect information only when you give it to us: a form, an application, a gift or an email.
  • We never sell, trade or rent your information.
  • This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies of its own.
  • You can ask to see, correct or remove your information at any time.

Information you give us

When you fill in a form on this site we collect what you enter. That can include your name, email address, phone number, postal address and, for program applications, passport details, background, health and emergency contact information. Our forms:

  • DTS inquiry and application, team booking and newsletter sign-up run on FormAssembly and are stored in our Salesforce database.
  • Tenderloin Connect volunteer sign-up runs on its own sign-up form.
  • Volunteering and general questions are sent by email from your own email app.
  • Fee payments requested through the Pay page are sent to our finance team by email.

If you fill in a reference for someone applying to one of our programs, we keep your details and your answers with their application.

Gifts and payments

Online gifts are processed by Click & Pledge on a secure form embedded on our Give page. Your card details go straight to Click & Pledge and are never seen or stored by this website. We receive a record of your gift for our accounts and to send your tax receipt.

Applications

Information that you or your referees give us when applying for one of our programs may be entered into the University of the Nations (UofN) database. YWAM San Francisco operates under and is certified by the UofN, and your information may be recorded there to register you as a UofN student.

Things this website loads from other services

  • Weather: the home page asks Open-Meteo for San Francisco's current weather. Like any website request, this shares your device's IP address with Open-Meteo, but nothing about you.
  • Videos: films play from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which doesn't set cookies until you press play.
  • Map: the Contact page shows a Google map.
  • Accreditation badges: the ECFA and GuideStar (Candid) badges load from those organizations.

The "Ask us anything" chat runs entirely in your browser: what you type is not sent anywhere or stored.

How we use information

  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To process applications to our programs.
  • To process gifts and payments, send receipts and keep accounting records.
  • To send newsletters and event invites, if you've signed up. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

We don't share your information except as described here, or when we tell you first and give you a chance to opt out. We use the services named above to run our forms, giving and email. If the law requires it, we will disclose information to government or law enforcement authorities, and we may share information to investigate or prevent fraud or illegal activity.

Other websites

Links to other websites, such as YouTube, Instagram or other YWAM locations, lead to sites with their own privacy policies, which this policy doesn't cover.

Keeping it safe

We protect the information we receive with physical, electronic and procedural safeguards, and this website is served only over a secure connection. No transmission over the internet is completely without risk.

Seeing, changing or removing your information

To see the information we hold about you, correct it, opt out of any use, or make a complaint, contact us. We aim to resolve complaints quickly.
